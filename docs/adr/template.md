# ADR-NNNN: Short title in the imperative

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded by [ADR-NNNN](NNNN-….md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** role or team, not individual names
- **Related:** [ADR-NNNN](NNNN-….md)

## Context

The forces at play: the constraint, the requirement, the thing that made this a
decision rather than a default. Write this before the Decision. If the context
does not make the decision feel close to inevitable, something is missing.

State facts, not preferences. "EKS costs $73/month per cluster and the budget is
$0" is context. "EKS is heavyweight" is an opinion.

## Decision

One paragraph, active voice, present tense. "We use X for Y."

## Alternatives considered

### Alternative A

What it was, and the specific reason it lost. Not "more complex" — *which*
complexity, and why it mattered here.

### Alternative B

As above.

## Consequences

### Positive

- What becomes possible or cheaper.

### Negative

- What becomes harder, slower or more expensive. **Fill this in.** An ADR with
  an empty negative section will not be believed, and the omission will be
  discovered at the worst time.

### Neutral

- What simply changes: new conventions, new operational steps.

## Compliance

How the decision is enforced, and what would detect a violation. A decision with
no enforcement is a preference.

## References

- Links to documentation, benchmarks, pricing pages, or the discussion.
